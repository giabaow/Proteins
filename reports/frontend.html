Question 3

What Proteins.1 should do

One consolidated recommendation, synthesised from the playbooks of Refeyn, Oxford Nanopore Technologies, Depixus.

The core move

Commercialise the enzyme-free single-molecule platform first as a research-use instrument for rare/low-abundance biomarker detection, proving superiority over bulk-average incumbents with named benchmarking partners, before using that credibility and revenue base to bridge into regulated diagnostics.

Applications to pursue

  • Ultra-sensitive oncology biomarker discovery (rare circulating proteins, early-detection panels) All three peers (Refeyn, ONT, Depixus) built their initial commercial base on single-molecule sensitivity beating bulk methods for low-abundance targets; oncology is the highest-value, most fundable entry point.
  • Neurology biomarker panels (neurodegenerative disease protein signatures) Oxford Nanopore's MATRIX CNS-tumour and VCGS ISO15189 path shows how a neurology/CNS beachhead can later anchor a clinical accreditation partnership.
  • Drug-discovery target validation and hit-to-lead biomolecular interaction profiling Depixus built its research-use GTM entirely around pharma target validation, positioning MAGNA One head-to-head against SPR/Biacore for interaction data at single-molecule resolution.
  • Multi-modal single-run DNA/RNA/protein biomarker panels ('one-platform' multiomic pitch) Refeyn and ONT both differentiate on replacing multiple bulk assays with one richer single-molecule readout; Proteins.1's cross-biomolecule capability is a natural extension of this positioning.
  • Biopharma QC/characterization (proteins, antibodies, viral vectors) as a later expansion Refeyn's trajectory from academic protein characterization into AAV/antibody GMP QC shows a natural second wave once instruments and software modules mature.

Market-route sequence

  1. 01
    Anchor scientific credibility on VTT IP and named academic authorityPublish the seminal enzyme-free physics paper and align a recognized academic co-founder/authority figure with the platform, mirroring Refeyn (Kukura), ONT, and Depixus (Croquette/Bensimon) precedents to build early trust.
  2. 02
    Publish head-to-head benchmarking studies against incumbentsRun and publish comparisons vs. Simoa, ELISA, mass spectrometry, SPR and AUC with named CRO/pharma/academic partners, following Refeyn's ABL Europe and Depixus's SPR-comparison playbooks to convert skepticism into adoption.
  3. 03
    Launch a tiered benchtop instrument line for research-use-only marketsOffer an entry-level and an automated/higher-throughput configuration from day one (as with Refeyn's TwoMP/SamuxMP and ONT's pocket-to-production scaling), targeting academic core facilities and CROs first.
  4. 04
    Fund via a blended non-dilutive + staged dilutive pathCombine EU/Horizon and national deep-tech grants with seed and Series A/B rounds from specialist life-science/deep-tech investors (as all three peers did), preserving runway for multi-year hardware/chemistry iteration.
  5. 05
    Build application-specific software modules and recruit commercial leadershipDevelop workflow-specific analysis modules per biomarker class (DNA/RNA/protein), and bring in a life-sciences-tools commercial executive with prior single-molecule/instrument scaling experience once core science is validated.
  6. 06
    Secure a flagship clinical/diagnostics partnership as the bridge to regulated useAfter building a research-use revenue base and reference customers, pursue an ISO15189-style accreditation partnership or landmark clinical lab collaboration (ONT/VCGS/MATRIX model) to move from research tool to regulated diagnostic.
Over time

First, establish scientific credibility through VTT-anchored IP, a named academic authority, and published benchmarking data against gold-standard incumbents (ELISA, mass spec, Simoa, SPR, AUC). Simultaneously launch a tiered research-use instrument (entry and automated versions) into academic core facilities, CROs, and pharma R&D for oncology, neurology, and drug-discovery target-validation applications, funded through a blended grant-plus-staged-VC path. As adoption and reference data accumulate, layer in application-specific software modules and recruit an experienced life-sciences-tools commercial leader to scale go-to-market and expand into pharma QC/characterization use cases. Only once a solid research-use revenue base and published validation record exist should Proteins.1 pursue a landmark clinical/diagnostics partnership (e.g., ISO15189 accreditation) as the deliberate bridge into regulated diagnostics, rather than attempting clinical entry prematurely.
